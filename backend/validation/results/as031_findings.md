# AS-031 findings — tracklet quality features (descriptive)

Source: `as031_quality_features.json` / `.md`, generated by
`python -m app.validation.quality` on the four frozen AS-022 fields with
the experimental default config and the frozen AS-022 SkyBoT snapshot.
Feature definitions: `docs/tracklet_quality_features.md`.

This is **hypothesis generation, not classifier validation**. Nothing here
is a threshold, score or rank.

## Data availability

- All listed features could be computed. `sharp` is not in the domain
  `SourceDetection` (dropped by design in `catalog_service._map_psf_row`);
  the evidence run supplies the raw catalog values and got a finite
  `sharp` for every detection of every tracklet (`complete` for all
  6362 tracklets). In the production/API path `sharp` stays `unavailable`.
- Raw `flags`: the only negative value in the four fields is −1 (edge;
  102 / 98 / 454 / 1146 rows), so no flag information is lost in the
  flags → `on_image_edge`/`mask_bits` mapping on this data.
- Every tracklet has exactly 3 detections (3-frame fields), so
  `detection_count` carries no information here.
- Reproducibility: two full runs gave identical features and summaries
  (only download/build timings differ); the 1995 DH record is checked by
  an offline test (re-extraction from the stored tracklet) and a live
  test (full POC re-run == committed record).

1. **SNR.** The KNOWN groups sit higher than the UNKNOWN groups; the
   median min SNR of validation targets is 14.1, of D unknowns 3.5. Within
   the crowded D field alone (same frames, same depth) KNOWN 10.8 vs
   UNKNOWN 3.5.
2. **Fit residual.** Median fit rms 0.05–0.10″ for KNOWN groups vs
   0.23–0.28″ for UNKNOWN built tracklets in every field; within D 0.085″
   vs 0.251″. The UNKNOWN distributions extend to the 0.5″ max-residual
   limit that defines "built", the KNOWN ones do not come close.
3. **Magnitude consistency.** Validation targets have a *larger* median
   range in σ (5.1) than D unknowns (1.8): bright targets have small
   photometric errors, so real asteroid brightness changes (rotation) and
   the missing colour term show up as many σ. In mag the
   ranges are similar (0.58 vs 0.66 mag). Range-in-σ is therefore not a
   simple consistency measure across brightness, and 10 of the 16 targets
   are in the mixed-filter fields B and C (see bias section).
4. **Field B unknowns** differ from all other groups: 68 of 88 have all
   three detections masked (65 with mask unions 4353 or 4097, i.e. bits 0
   and 12 with or without bit 8; 3 with bit 8 only), higher min SNR (6.8 vs 3.9 for the other B
   unknowns), large magnitude ranges (partly the zg/zr filter change) and
   positive `sharp` (median max 0.58 vs 0.24). Their position angles are spread over all directions, so they
   are not one straight streak.
5. **Crowded D unknowns** (833 built, 4792 rejected): 197 built tracklets
   have all three detections flagged (masked or on the image edge); 177 of
   them have all three masked, mostly bit 12. (Corrected in AS-032: this
   line first said "masked" for all 197.) Position angles are
   close to uniform. Built and rejected D unknowns have almost the same
   SNR, magnitude and sharp distributions; they differ in fit residual,
   which is the rejection criterion itself.
6. **Flags on real objects.** 1 of 16 validation targets (80429, field D)
   has one detection with mask bit 12; no KNOWN tracklet has an edge
   detection.
7. **Speed** medians are 0.58–0.70″/min in every group: the 1″/min rate
   limit and the main-belt-dominated target selection make this
   uninformative here. Position angle is not pooled (circular, depends on
   the field's ecliptic geometry).

## Selection effects and bias

- **Brightness selection.** Validation targets are pre-registered with
  V ≤ maglimit − 0.5; they are bright by construction. Higher SNR and
  smaller fit residuals of KNOWN tracklets partly restate that selection
  (astrometric error scales with SNR), not a property of "real" vs
  "false" tracklets.
- **Identification needs a match within 2″** in every frame; poorly fitted
  or faint tracklets are less likely to be identified even if real.
- **UNKNOWN is a mixture**: real unknown or not-in-SkyBoT objects,
  mislinked real sources, and artefact combinations. Its distributions are
  not those of a clean "false" class.
- **Crowding/depth**: D has ~10× the sources of the other fields and
  contributes 833 of 960 built unknowns; pooled unknown statistics are
  dominated by one field. Within-field comparisons (D: KNOWN vs UNKNOWN)
  reduce but do not remove this.
- **Small numbers**: 16 validation targets, 9 KNOWN in D, 1 control;
  percentiles of such groups are rough.
- **Filters differ between frames in B (zg, zr, zr) and C (zr, zi, zg)**;
  POC and D are zr only. With the colour term set to 0, magnitude ranges
  in B and C include the source's colour, so they are not comparable with
  POC/D and pooled magnitude statistics mix both cases.

## Next research hypotheses (not implemented)

- H1: Within one field, min SNR and fit rms separate KNOWN from UNKNOWN
  tracklets beyond what the target brightness selection explains. Test:
  compare KNOWN non-target objects of matched V magnitude with unknowns.
- H2: The masked, high-`sharp`, large-magnitude-range tracklets in B (and
  the all-masked ones in D) are artefact-driven. Test: blink-review a
  sample in the AS-029/030 viewer; look up the mask bit meanings (bits 0,
  8, 12) in the ZSDS Explanatory Supplement §10.3.
- H3: Magnitude consistency should be measured relative to photometric
  error *and* expected asteroid variability (range in σ grows with SNR).
  A per-tracklet χ² against a constant magnitude is a candidate measure.
- H4: A 4-frame / K-of-N sequence would make `detection_count` and
  per-detection consistency informative (research backlog item).
